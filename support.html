MOFO extension · help

Something broken or confusing? Email mindoverfomotools@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a day or two. Tell us your browser, the token page you were on and what you saw. A screenshot helps a lot.

Quick fixes

  • The panel doesn't show up. MOFO only runs on fomo.family token pages. Reload the tab once after installing or updating the extension, and make sure you're logged in to fomo.family.
  • Holder data says "retry in a few seconds". The holder data source limits how fast it can be asked. MOFO waits and tries again by itself.
  • "Daily limit reached". The free service has a daily limit per install for Ask MOFO and holder data. It resets every day.
  • The chart colors look wrong. Pick black and white, green and red or fomo's own colors in MOFO settings (click the MOFO toolbar icon).
  • Want to see how it works first? Open MOFO settings and click "See a demo on sample data".

Your data

Everything MOFO saves stays in your browser, and removing the extension deletes it. To delete your THE CALL data from our server, email us your caller code (MOFO settings, THE CALL section). Details are in the privacy policy.
